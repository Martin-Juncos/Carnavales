## Specs

- S1. “si lo apruebo” (user approved `docs/voting/resultados-diseno.md` on 2026-11-24). Motor v1 (`GOYA_2026_V1`) computes results only on `CLOSED` non-retired editions from scoring competitive nights (`cuenta_para_puntaje = true`, official receipts of frozen version) and active `D14` penalties.
- S2. Migration `0028-resultados-publicacion.sql` creates immutable/append-only `calculo_resultado`, `resultado_comparsa`, `resultado_rubro_sujeto`, and `empate_resultado` tables with `BEFORE UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE` triggers, `ESCRIBANO`-only writes, `CLOSED`-edition guard, unique partial published snapshot per edition, tie-break publication blocker (`EMPATES_PENDIENTES`), atomic audit (`resultado_calculado`, `empate_resuelto`, `resultado_publicado`), and full `eliminar_edicion_completa` (`0025`) compatibility.
- S3. Calculation engine (`D24`, `D6/R9`, `D13`, `D14`, `D25`, `A10`): hoja sum + parent-sum of children, `EXCESO_SHOW_BATERIA` on its rubric (floor 0.00), `PRIMERA` general ranking = fixed + best nominative per comparsa + top `maxCarrosComparsa` (default 2) carros minus general penalties (floor 0.00), `MINIMO_INTEGRANTES` disqualifies comparsa general prizes while keeping individual rubric prizes, `AGRUPACION` only in rubric prizes, `ALEATORIO` and `suma_a_comparsa = false` excluded from general ranking, position 1 tie detection on general ranking and every root rubric (`padreId IS NULL`).
- S4. API routes: `POST /api/escribano/ediciones/:edicionId/resultados/calcular`, `POST /api/escribano/ediciones/:edicionId/resultados/:calculoId/empates/:empateId/resolver`, `POST /api/escribano/ediciones/:edicionId/resultados/:calculoId/publicar` (`ESCRIBANO`, `{ confirmar: true }`, idempotent, `Cache-Control: no-store`); and post-closure read routes `GET /api/resultados/ediciones/:edicionId` and `GET /api/resultados/ediciones/:edicionId/puntajes` (`ESCRIBANO` or `ADMINISTRADOR` only, `403` for `JURADO`, `409 edicion_no_cerrada` before total closure).
- S5. Client UI: **Resultados y publicación** in `PanelEscribano` (calculate, general & rubric tables, tie resolution modal, publish modal blocked while ties pending, snapshot history) and **Ver resultados** read-only view in `PanelEdiciones` for `CLOSED` editions, plus synced docs (`docs/voting/resultados-api.md`, `docs/voting/resultados-diseno.md`, `docs/README.md`) and full + independent verification.
